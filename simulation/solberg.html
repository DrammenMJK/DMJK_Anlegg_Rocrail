Solberg station

Experimental, offline simulation from 20261119232535091-DMJK-Layout.xml. Set turnouts, choose a direction, then press a panel switch. Green tracks connect station blocks, drawing edges or a buffer stop; they are not locked routes or permission to drive. Turnouts start straight and signals red.

Panel, output, turnout, sensor and signal actions are replayed without timing. Route locking and other action types are not simulated; detectors start from the XML snapshot and have no controls here. This is not Rocrail or live status. Hover for XML details and addresses; LS100 and OD-2 assignments are from DMJK-Adresser.pdf. Open a module type and click a module to highlight its objects.
